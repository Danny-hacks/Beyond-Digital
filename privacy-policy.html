Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: January 2026

Who we are

Beyond Digital ("we", "us", "our") is a digital marketing and web development agency based at Les Allées, D'Helvetia, Moka, Mauritius. This policy explains how we handle personal information collected through beyonddigital.mu and in the course of providing our services.

Information we collect

We collect information you give us directly, such as when you submit a quote request, fill in our contact form, email us, or message us on WhatsApp. This may include your name, email address, phone number, business details, and the content of your message.

Like most websites, our server and hosting provider may automatically log basic technical information (such as browser type, device, and pages visited) to keep the site secure and understand how it's used. We may also use analytics tools, such as Google Analytics, for this purpose.

How we use your information

  • To respond to your enquiry and prepare quotes or proposals
  • To deliver the web design, marketing, or AI services you've engaged us for
  • To send project-related updates and, occasionally, relevant news about our services
  • To improve our website and understand how visitors use it

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

Sharing your information

We may share information with trusted service providers who help us run our business — for example, our hosting provider, email service, or analytics provider — solely for the purpose of delivering our services. These providers are only given the information needed to perform their function.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies to remember preferences and understand site usage. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings; doing so may affect how parts of the site function.

Data retention

We keep personal information only for as long as needed to respond to your enquiry, deliver our services, or meet our legal and accounting obligations, after which it is deleted or anonymised.

Your rights

Under the Mauritius Data Protection Act 2017, and applicable data protection law if you're contacting us from the UK, EU, or elsewhere, you have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal information, and to object to or restrict certain processing. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.
